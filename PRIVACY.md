# Doc Alchemist — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 30 September 2026*

Doc Alchemist is a desktop app that converts documents between
Markdown, Word and PDF. It is built so that your documents never leave
your computer.

## What the app does with your data

- **Your documents stay on your device.** Files you open are converted
  locally, and the result is saved next to the original. Nothing is
  uploaded, stored or shared by the app.
- **No accounts, no tracking.** The app has no sign-in, no analytics,
  no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not collect or store
  personal information.

## When the app uses the internet

The app only goes online in these cases:

1. **"Check for updates"**: only when you click it. The app then asks
   `api.github.com` for the latest versions of Doc Alchemist and pandoc,
   and `pypi.org` for the latest version of markitdown. The Microsoft
   Store version does not check for app updates (the Store does that),
   but it still checks the versions of these components.
2. **Images linked from a document**: if a Markdown file you convert
   refers to an image by web address (for example
   `![logo](https://example.com/logo.png)`), the image is downloaded from
   that address so it can be placed in the output document.

In both cases, the servers contacted receive the standard information
included in any web request, such as your IP address. They never
receive your documents or their contents. Those services are run by
third parties (GitHub, the Python Software Foundation, and whoever hosts
a linked image) under their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.
